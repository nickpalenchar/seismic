# Changelog

What changes for Seismic's users, version by version, newest first. Each entry links its demo.
How a version's section is written: [`docs/release.md`](docs/release.md).

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-17

The first release of Seismic as its own app, for macOS, Windows and Linux: direct AI coding agents
from one canvas, by voice or from your phone, and approve their work from the demos they record.
From this version on, the app offers its own updates.

### New

- **Cards on a canvas.** Tasks, ideas and plans in groups, on as many canvases as a project needs, with dependency arrows that decide what starts first ([demo](https://nickpalenchar.github.io/obeya-demos/manually-connect-cards-with-dependency-a-fe8ef3/)).
- **A Table view** lists a project's cards in rows, as the other way to see the canvas ([demo](http://demos.seismic.im/projects-should-have-a-table-view-e43a75/)).
- **Many agents at once**, each in its own clone or worktree, with every workstation in the status bar ([demo](http://demos.seismic.im/display-options-for-many-workstations-1d1005/)).
- **A demo for every change**: watch it, approve it or give feedback; the code waits in its own Diff tab ([demo](http://demos.seismic.im/card-diff-tab-file-tree-and-files-collap-23c4ca/)).
- **Code reviews** by a second agent: fix each finding, make it a follow-up or ignore it ([demo](http://demos.seismic.im/on-follow-up-reviews-the-status-should-g-32e14d/)).
- **Pick the agent and model** for each card: Claude Code, or Codex as a preview ([demo](https://nickpalenchar.github.io/obeya-demos/choose-a-model-at-start-c6d2d7/)).
- **Usage limits**: agents pause at the limit and carry on after the reset ([demo](https://nickpalenchar.github.io/obeya-demos/pause-agents-on-session-limit-and-auto-w-3c20e5/)).
- **Chat with Seismic**: the cards it creates show in the conversation ([demo](http://demos.seismic.im/chat-should-embed-created-cards-in-separ-4d5dc9/)).
- **Merges in planning**: cards that would clash are proposed as one ([demo](http://demos.seismic.im/merge-proposals-should-show-both-cards-c-6a81be/)).
- **Unfinished changes** in your checkout become a task an agent finishes ([demo](http://demos.seismic.im/suggest-finishing-working-changes-on-new-c122b5/)).
- **A phone view**, one project at a time, with a full-page chat and card search ([demo](http://demos.seismic.im/the-new-shell-under-view-phone-next-aea68b/)).
- **A three-step welcome** checks your machine and sets up your first project ([demo](http://demos.seismic.im/onboarding-restructure-welcome-dialog-to-f0a0dd/)).
- **Card labels**: category, sub-category, complexity and priority, suggested for you ([demo](https://nickpalenchar.github.io/obeya-demos/labels-in-the-data-and-the-api-be1a1d/)).
- **Schema diagrams** beside the demo of a change to an app's database ([demo](https://nickpalenchar.github.io/obeya-demos/add-visualized-schema-change-artifact-to-3be8dc/)).
